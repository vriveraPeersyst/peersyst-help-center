Want your XRP in a wallet that is truly yours? Let's set one up in under a minute.

Prefer to watch? The video walkthrough runs 57 seconds.

## 1. Open XRP Mobile

![Open XRP Mobile](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-1.png)

Open XRP Mobile. It's a self custody wallet, so the keys stay with you, not with an exchange.

## 2. Tap Create wallet

![Tap Create wallet](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-2.png)

Tap Create wallet. You could import one you already have, but today we start fresh.

## 3. Back up your recovery phrase

![Back up your recovery phrase](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-3.png)

Tap to reveal your recovery phrase, then write the 12 words down in order and store them somewhere safe. Anyone with these words can take your funds, so never share them.

## 4. Name it and set your PIN

![Name it and set your PIN, screen 1 of 2](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-4-1.png)

![Name it and set your PIN, screen 2 of 2](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-4-2.png)

Give your wallet a name if you like, then set a 4 digit PIN. You'll use it to unlock the app and sign transactions.

## 5. Wallet created

![Wallet created](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-5.png)

And that's it, your XRPL account is ready. Tap Open wallet.

## 6. You're all set

![You're all set, screen 1 of 2](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-6-1.png)

![You're all set, screen 2 of 2](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-6-2.png)

Allow notifications if you want to know when funds arrive, and welcome home: buy, send, receive and swap XRP, right from your pocket.
