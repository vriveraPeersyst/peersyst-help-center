Want your XRP in a wallet that is truly yours? Let's set one up in under a minute.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 8 screens and 50 seconds of video.

## 1. Open XRP Mobile

![Open XRP Mobile](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-1.png)

Open XRP Mobile. It's a self custody wallet, so the keys stay on your phone, not with an exchange.

## 2. Tap Create wallet

![Tap Create wallet](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-2.png)

Tap Create wallet. You could import one you already have, but today we start fresh.

## 3. Reveal and write down your 12 words

![Reveal and write down your 12 words](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-3.png)

Tap to reveal your recovery phrase, then write the 12 words down in order and keep them somewhere safe. Anyone who has them can take your funds, and nobody from XRP Mobile will ever ask you for them.

## 4. Name your wallet

![Name your wallet](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-4.png)

Give the wallet a name so you can tell it apart later. You can change it whenever you like.

## 5. Set a 4 digit PIN, twice

![Set a 4 digit PIN, twice](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-5.png)

Now set a 4 digit PIN and confirm it. You'll use it to unlock the app and to sign every transaction.

## 6. Notifications are your call

![Notifications are your call](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-6.png)

Turn on notifications to stay on top of the latest updates, or skip it and decide later in settings.

## 7. Wallet created

![Wallet created](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-7.png)

And that's your XRP Ledger account ready. Tap Open wallet.

## 8. Enter your referral code

![Enter your referral code](/help/xrp-mobile/create-wallet/step-8.png)

Got a referral code? Type it in here to join the campaign.
