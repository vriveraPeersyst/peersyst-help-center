Turn XRP into a dollar stablecoin without an exchange account, without signing up anywhere. Here is how the swap works, and exactly what it costs.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/swap/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/swap/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 8 screens and 92 seconds of video.

## 1. Tap Swap

![Tap Swap](/help/xrp-mobile/swap/step-1.png)

From the wallet home, tap Swap. You pick what you are paying with on top and what you want underneath: XRP for USDC here, and the arrows in the middle flip the pair.

## 2. Type the amount

![Type the amount](/help/xrp-mobile/swap/step-2.png)

Type what you want to swap, or take a percentage of your balance. The provider sets a minimum, two XRP at the time of recording, and the field tells you when you are under it.

## 3. Read the quote

![Read the quote](/help/xrp-mobile/swap/step-3.png)

The quote fills in as you type: what you get, in USDC and in your own currency, and the rate it is based on. It is a live quote, not a promise, which is why it refreshes.

## 4. Check the terms

![Check the terms](/help/xrp-mobile/swap/step-4.png)

The second step is where the real cost is. Two fees: the network fee, a hundredth of a cent, and the provider's, nought point eight eight percent. Max slippage of one percent is the worst rate you accept: if the market moves further than that while it settles, the swap fails instead of filling badly.

## 5. Sign with your PIN

![Sign with your PIN](/help/xrp-mobile/swap/step-5.png)

Confirm and swap, then your PIN. Your keys sign this on the phone, the same as any payment.

## 6. It crosses two networks

![It crosses two networks](/help/xrp-mobile/swap/step-6.png)

This is not a trade on the XRP Ledger. Your XRP leaves the ledger and the swap is routed through NEAR Intents, which is why it takes half a minute instead of four seconds.

## 7. Two hashes, both pending

![Two hashes, both pending](/help/xrp-mobile/swap/step-7.png)

The screen shows both legs while they settle: the intents hash and the NEAR transactions. Pending means it is still moving, not that it is stuck. Leaving the app does not cancel it.

## 8. Swapped

![Swapped](/help/xrp-mobile/swap/step-8.png)

Done. The hashes are now real, and your USDC balance is on the wallet home. Go again if you want to swap the rest.
