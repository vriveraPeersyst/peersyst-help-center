Your wallet does not have to be in English. XRP Mobile ships in 13 languages, and switching takes two taps.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/app-language/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/app-language/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 4 screens and 19 seconds of video.

## 1. Open Settings

![Open Settings](/help/xrp-mobile/app-language/step-1.png)

Tap the gear, and stay on General.

## 2. Tap App language

![Tap App language](/help/xrp-mobile/app-language/step-2.png)

Tap App language. This changes the app only. It does not touch your wallet, your keys or your balances.

## 3. Pick yours

![Pick yours](/help/xrp-mobile/app-language/step-3.png)

Pick yours from the list. Thirteen languages ship with the app, translated in full.

## 4. The whole app follows

![The whole app follows](/help/xrp-mobile/app-language/step-4.png)

And the whole interface comes back in it, right away, with nothing to restart.
