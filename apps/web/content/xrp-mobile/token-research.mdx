Before you put money into a ticker, spend thirty seconds finding out what it actually is. The app has the page for it.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/token-research/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/token-research/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 4 screens and 32 seconds of video.

## 1. Open it from the market

![Open it from the market](/help/xrp-mobile/token-research/step-1.png)

In Explore, tap any row. Every listed token has its own page, whether or not you hold it.

## 2. Position is what you hold

![Position is what you hold](/help/xrp-mobile/token-research/step-2.png)

It opens on Position, which is your own balance in that token. On a fresh wallet that is a row of zeros, so move across to Investor Info.

## 3. The chart

![The chart](/help/xrp-mobile/token-research/step-3.png)

Now the candles, and a price you can read against a real scale instead of a single number.

## 4. Then the boring numbers

![Then the boring numbers](/help/xrp-mobile/token-research/step-4.png)

And under it the numbers that actually matter: market cap, 24 hour volume and total supply, with links to the project's own site, its X account and its report on Messari.
