Did that payment actually go through? Every transaction your wallet has ever made or received is one tap away, with the receipt that settles the argument.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/activity-history/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/activity-history/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 5 screens and 55 seconds of video.

## 1. Balances, then history

![Balances, then history](/help/xrp-mobile/activity-history/step-1.png)

The wallet home has two tabs. Currencies is what you hold right now. Activity is how it got there.

## 2. Everything in and out

![Everything in and out](/help/xrp-mobile/activity-history/step-2.png)

Every payment, sent and received, newest first, with the amount in XRP and in your own currency. The tiny incoming amounts you do not recognise are spam: the XRP Ledger lets anyone send you a fraction of a cent, and some senders use it to advertise. Ignore them, and never follow a link one of them carries.

## 3. Open one for the receipt

![Open one for the receipt](/help/xrp-mobile/activity-history/step-3.png)

Tap any entry to open its receipt.

## 4. Fee, status and hash

![Fee, status and hash](/help/xrp-mobile/activity-history/step-4.png)

Who it went to, what the network charged, the date, and whether it completed. At the bottom, the transaction hash: that is the proof. Copy it, or open it in the explorer to see the same payment on the public ledger, from outside the app.

## 5. Back to your balances

![Back to your balances](/help/xrp-mobile/activity-history/step-5.png)

Back out and you are on your balances again. Nothing here is stored by us: the list is read from the XRP Ledger every time you open it.
