Buying crypto with a card is easy. Knowing what it costs you is the part nobody shows. XRP Mobile puts the whole bill on one screen before you commit.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/buy-xrp/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/buy-xrp/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 5 screens and 37 seconds of video.

## 1. Tap Buy

![Tap Buy](/help/xrp-mobile/buy-xrp/step-1.png)

From the wallet home, tap Buy. The XRP lands straight in this wallet, at the address you already have.

## 2. Pick an amount

![Pick an amount](/help/xrp-mobile/buy-xrp/step-2.png)

Pick an amount, or type your own. Underneath it tells you roughly how much XRP that becomes at today's price.

## 3. Change it, the quote follows

![Change it, the quote follows](/help/xrp-mobile/buy-xrp/step-3.png)

Change your mind and everything recalculates, because this is a live quote from the provider rather than a rough estimate.

## 4. Read the whole bill

![Read the whole bill](/help/xrp-mobile/buy-xrp/step-4.png)

Here is the part worth reading: the rate, the provider's fee, exactly how much XRP reaches you, and how long it takes.

## 5. The next tap leaves the app

![The next tap leaves the app](/help/xrp-mobile/buy-xrp/step-5.png)

Continue hands you to the payment provider for the card details, outside XRP Mobile. Your card never touches the wallet, and neither do we.
