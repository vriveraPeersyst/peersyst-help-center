## Start here

- **Stuck on something?** Go to [Common issues](./issues): each page describes the problem the way people describe it, explains what is going on, and tells you whether you can fix it yourself or need to talk to us.
- **Want to learn how to do something?** Every guide is the written twin of a video tutorial: the same steps, one screenshot per step, and the video embedded at the top.
- **Quick question?** The [FAQ](./faq) has one page per question, so you can send a link.

## Two things no guide will ever ask you

XRP Mobile is self custody. Your recovery phrase, secret numbers and private key never leave your phone, and nobody from XRP Mobile or Peersyst will ever ask you for them. Not in the support chat, not by email, not on social media. Anyone who does is trying to steal from you.

## Talk to a person

Open the app, tap the gear, move to **Support** and tap **Start a chat**. If the app will not open, you can still reach the same team from the [get support](./get-support) guide.
