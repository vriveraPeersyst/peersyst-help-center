Before you buy anything, look at what is actually moving. The Explore tab is the market, inside your wallet.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/explore-market/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/explore-market/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 4 screens and 26 seconds of video.

## 1. Open Explore

![Open Explore](/help/xrp-mobile/explore-market/step-1.png)

Tap Explore. No account, no sign up: this is just the market, and you can read it all day without owning anything.

## 2. Top movers first

![Top movers first](/help/xrp-mobile/explore-market/step-2.png)

Top movers opens with the biggest gainers and losers of the last 24 hours, which is usually the only question anyone actually has.

## 3. Then every token

![Then every token](/help/xrp-mobile/explore-market/step-3.png)

Under it sits every token the app lists, with its price and its move on the day.

## 4. Keep going

![Keep going](/help/xrp-mobile/explore-market/step-4.png)

Keep scrolling, or search by name or symbol, and tap any token for its own page and chart.
