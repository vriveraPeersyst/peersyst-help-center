There is an Earn tab in XRP Mobile, and right now it earns you nothing. Here is why that is deliberate.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/earn-notify/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/earn-notify/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 4 screens and 29 seconds of video.

## 1. Open Earn

![Open Earn](/help/xrp-mobile/earn-notify/step-1.png)

Tap Earn. No rate, no launch date, no fine print, because there is no product yet.

## 2. What we are aiming for

![What we are aiming for](/help/xrp-mobile/earn-notify/step-2.png)

Instead it says what we are building towards: XRP that works while it sits, keys that never leave your phone, and no lock ups.

## 3. Where we would start

![Where we would start](/help/xrp-mobile/earn-notify/step-3.png)

And where we would start: XRP first, then RLUSD for balances you would rather keep in dollars. Nothing confirmed, no rate promised.

## 4. Get told when it lands

![Get told when it lands](/help/xrp-mobile/earn-notify/step-4.png)

If you want to know the day it goes live, turn on the notification and forget about it.
