Something is not working and you want a person. Before that, the app already answers the questions we get asked most.

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 4 screens and 32 seconds of video.

## 1. Settings, then Support

![Settings, then Support](/help/xrp-mobile/get-support/step-1.png)

Open the gear and move to Support. Everything about getting help lives on this one tab.

## 2. Chat with the team

![Chat with the team](/help/xrp-mobile/get-support/step-2.png)

Start a chat puts you through to the team. Nobody there will ever ask for your recovery phrase or your secret numbers, and anyone who does is stealing from you.

## 3. The 1 XRP question

![The 1 XRP question](/help/xrp-mobile/get-support/step-3.png)

Under it sits the FAQ, and it opens with the question we get more than any other: why 1 XRP is locked in your wallet.

## 4. Swaps, chains and waiting

![Swaps, chains and waiting](/help/xrp-mobile/get-support/step-4.png)

Then how swaps work, where your Bitcoin and Ethereum are actually held, why a deposit takes a few minutes, and whether you can receive from other networks.
