Sending XRP to an exchange? There is one field that decides whether it reaches your account there or sits in limbo, and it is hidden until you ask for it.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 12 screens and 98 seconds of video.

## 1. Tap Send

![Tap Send](/help/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag/step-1.png)

Start the same way as any payment: from the wallet home, tap Send.

## 2. Choose the recipient

![Choose the recipient](/help/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag/step-2.png)

Tap the address field to open the recipient sheet.

## 3. Type or scan the address

![Type or scan the address](/help/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag/step-3.png)

Type the exchange's deposit address, or scan its QR. This is the address of the exchange itself, shared by thousands of customers.

## 4. Green means it checks out

![Green means it checks out](/help/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag/step-4.png)

Green means the address is valid, and that is exactly the problem: a valid exchange address on its own does not say which account inside the exchange the money belongs to.

## 5. Add destination tag

![Add destination tag](/help/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag/step-5.png)

That is what the destination tag is for. It stays hidden until you tap Add destination tag, so nobody sends one by accident.

## 6. Type the tag they gave you

![Type the tag they gave you](/help/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag/step-6.png)

Type the exact number the exchange gave you, on its deposit page, next to the address. It is your account number inside their wallet. Only add one when they ask for it: a tag sent to a personal wallet is simply ignored.

## 7. Then the amount

![Then the amount](/help/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag/step-7.png)

Then the amount, as usual, typed on the pad or taken from the percentage shortcuts.

## 8. Address and tag, side by side

![Address and tag, side by side](/help/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag/step-8.png)

Before you continue, read the address and the tag together. Both have to match the deposit page you copied them from.

## 9. The tag rides with the payment

![The tag rides with the payment](/help/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag/step-9.png)

The summary shows the tag on its own line. It travels inside the transaction, which is how the exchange knows to credit you and not somebody else.

## 10. Sign with your PIN

![Sign with your PIN](/help/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag/step-10.png)

Confirm and send, then your PIN. From here it is irreversible: an exchange deposit with the wrong tag becomes a support ticket, and sometimes not even that.

## 11. The ledger settles it

![The ledger settles it](/help/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag/step-11.png)

The XRP Ledger settles it in seconds. Crediting it on the other side is the exchange's job, and that can take a few minutes longer.

## 12. Sent, tag included

![Sent, tag included](/help/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag/step-12.png)

Done. Keep the transaction hash: if a deposit ever goes missing, that string plus the tag is what support will ask you for.
