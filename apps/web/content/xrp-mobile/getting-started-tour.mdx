You've just made a wallet and the app is staring back at you. Here is what every tab does, in under a minute.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/getting-started-tour/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/getting-started-tour/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 6 screens and 37 seconds of video.

## 1. Wallet is home

![Wallet is home](/help/xrp-mobile/getting-started-tour/step-1.png)

Wallet is where you land: your balance, the four things you can do with it, and your tokens and activity underneath.

## 2. Earn, still on the way

![Earn, still on the way](/help/xrp-mobile/getting-started-tour/step-2.png)

Earn is honest about itself. There is no yield product yet, just what we are building and a way to be told when it lands.

## 3. Explore is the market

![Explore is the market](/help/xrp-mobile/getting-started-tour/step-3.png)

Explore is the market: what is moving today, every token we list, and a page per token with its chart.

## 4. Share is the campaign

![Share is the campaign](/help/xrp-mobile/getting-started-tour/step-4.png)

Share is the referral campaign. Your points, the leaderboard, and the tasks that earn you a slice of the pot.

## 5. Settings behind the gear

![Settings behind the gear](/help/xrp-mobile/getting-started-tour/step-5.png)

The gear holds the rest: your currency, your language, your PIN, your backup, and support.

## 6. Back to your wallet

![Back to your wallet](/help/xrp-mobile/getting-started-tour/step-6.png)

And you are never more than one tap from your wallet.
