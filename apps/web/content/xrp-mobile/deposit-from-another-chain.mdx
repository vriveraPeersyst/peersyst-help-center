Your USDC is sitting on Base, or Solana, or Ethereum, and your wallet lives on the XRP Ledger. Here is how to bring it across, and the one mistake that loses it.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/deposit-from-another-chain/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/deposit-from-another-chain/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 5 screens and 52 seconds of video.

## 1. Tap Receive

![Tap Receive](/help/xrp-mobile/deposit-from-another-chain/step-1.png)

It starts where any deposit starts: Receive, on the wallet home.

## 2. Pick the asset

![Pick the asset](/help/xrp-mobile/deposit-from-another-chain/step-2.png)

The wallet holds more than XRP. Bitcoin, Ether, the stablecoins, Solana: pick the one that is coming in.

## 3. Pick the network

![Pick the network](/help/xrp-mobile/deposit-from-another-chain/step-3.png)

Then the network it is coming from, and this is the part that matters. The same USDC exists on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Solana and half a dozen others, and each one needs a different deposit address.

## 4. One asset, one network

![One asset, one network](/help/xrp-mobile/deposit-from-another-chain/step-4.png)

So read the warning. This address takes USDC on Base and nothing else. Send another token, or the right token on the wrong network, and it is gone: nobody can reverse it and nobody can recover it.

## 5. Your deposit address

![Your deposit address](/help/xrp-mobile/deposit-from-another-chain/step-5.png)

Copy the address or show the QR, and check it every single time. It is generated for this asset and this network, and it can change between deposits, so never reuse one you saved last month.
