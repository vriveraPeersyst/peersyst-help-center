Before that phone goes to anyone else, take the wallet off it. And understand exactly what that does, because there is no undo.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/delete-data/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/delete-data/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 3 screens and 25 seconds of video.

## 1. Settings, then Security

![Settings, then Security](/help/xrp-mobile/delete-data/step-1.png)

Open the gear and move to Security. Delete data sits at the bottom, in red, on its own.

## 2. What it removes

![What it removes](/help/xrp-mobile/delete-data/step-2.png)

It wipes the wallets, the keys and the PIN from this device. Your money is untouched: it never lived on the phone, it lives on the XRP Ledger.

## 3. There is no undo

![There is no undo](/help/xrp-mobile/delete-data/step-3.png)

Which is exactly why this is only safe if you still have your recovery phrase. Without it, nobody can bring the account back. Not you, not us.
