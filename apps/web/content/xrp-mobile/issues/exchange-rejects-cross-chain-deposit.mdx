## What is going on

When you send an asset to another network, the payment that lands at the destination is made by the intents layer on your behalf, not from an address the exchange has seen before. Most exchanges credit it normally. A few hold it for review or refuse "third party" deposits, and the app warns about this on the send screen.

XRP sent natively on the XRP Ledger does not have this problem.

## The fix

1. Copy the last hash from the transfer screen or from Activity, the one on the destination network.
2. Check it on that network's explorer: it should show the amount arriving at the exchange's address.
3. Open a ticket with the **exchange's support** with that hash, the amount and the time. They hold the funds and only they can credit them. It can take days.

## Avoiding it next time

- When the destination is an exchange, prefer sending XRP natively and swapping on the exchange.
- Or test with a small amount first on any new route to an exchange.
