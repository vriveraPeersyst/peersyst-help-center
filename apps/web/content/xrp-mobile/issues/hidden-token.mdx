## What is going on

Any token in the balance list can be hidden from its details screen, with the eye icon at the top right. A hidden token leaves the list, and also leaves the pickers in Swap and Send, so a swap that "only offers the same token twice" or a send that "cannot find the token" is almost always a token that was hidden, usually by accident while tapping around.

Your funds never moved. Hiding is a display setting on this phone.

## The fix

1. Go to the wallet home and scroll to the bottom of the balance list.
2. Tap **Show filtered tokens**.
3. Tap the token to open its details, then tap the **eye icon** at the top right to unhide it.
4. Go back. The token is in the list again and can be selected everywhere.

If the eye icon does not seem to react, tap it once more and check the list: it is easy to hide it again by tapping twice.
