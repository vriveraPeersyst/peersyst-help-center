## What is going on

The app reads your XRP balance from the XRP Ledger, your multichain balances from the intents layer, and prices from a market data provider. Any of the three can be slow or briefly unavailable, especially during market spikes when everyone refreshes at once. The app then shows a spinner, a zero or a stale number until the next successful load.

Nothing about this touches your funds. The ledger has them regardless of what the screen says.

## The fix

1. Pull down on the wallet home to refresh.
2. If that does not help, close the app completely and open it again.
3. Check your connection, and try again in a few minutes.
4. If you want certainty right now, look your address up on an XRP Ledger explorer such as xrpscan.com.

## A related symptom

During the same kind of congestion, importing a wallet can wrongly report the recovery phrase as invalid. Wait a few minutes and import again before doubting the words.

## If a token disappeared but everything else loads

That is a hidden token, not a loading problem: see [A token disappeared](/docs/xrp-mobile/issues/hidden-token).
