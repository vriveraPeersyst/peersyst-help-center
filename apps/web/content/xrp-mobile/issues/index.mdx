Each issue page opens with three things: **how it shows up** (in the words people use when they write to us), **who can fix it**, and **what to have ready** if you end up contacting support.

The "who can fix it" label means:

- **You can fix this yourself.** The steps on the page are the whole fix. The support agent will point you at them.
- **Support can resolve this in chat.** The steps need a little checking along the way. The support agent can walk you through them and confirm the outcome.
- **Needs a human from the team.** Something has to be looked up or corrected on our side. Gather the details listed on the page and contact support from Settings, then Support.
