## What is going on

Your XRP Ledger account must keep 1 XRP on the ledger to exist (the base reserve), and every payment costs a tiny network fee that comes out of your balance. So the most you can send is your balance minus 1 XRP minus the fee. The app's spendable balance and the **Max** button already do that arithmetic for you.

## The fix

On the amount screen tap **Max** instead of typing the number. If you typed a number and it fails, lower it by a hair; the fee is a hundredth of a cent, so the reserve is what is in the way.

## It fails without a clear message

A swap, a send or an activation that fails at the signing step with a generic error, while the balance looks sufficient, is usually the same thing: the amount plus the fee would cross the reserve. Keep a small margin of spendable XRP in the account, above the 1 XRP reserve, and try again.

## Emptying the wallet completely

The 1 XRP reserve stays as long as the account exists. Closing an account is a separate XRP Ledger operation that the app does not offer, so in practice the last 1 XRP stays put. For multichain assets there is no reserve: Max sends the whole balance.
