## What is going on

Your XRP Ledger account must keep 1 XRP on the ledger to exist (the base reserve), and every payment costs a tiny network fee that comes out of your balance. So the most you can send is your balance minus 1 XRP minus the fee. The app's spendable balance and the **Max** button already do that arithmetic for you.

## The fix

On the amount screen tap **Max** instead of typing the number. If you typed a number and it fails, lower it by a hair; the fee is a hundredth of a cent, so the reserve is what is in the way.

## Only XRP has a reserve

This applies to XRP on the XRP Ledger and to nothing else. Bitcoin, Ether, USDC and every other asset in the wallet are held through the intents layer, and swapping or sending them needs no XRP at all: no gas, no minimum balance. If a swap of a multichain asset fails, the reserve is not the reason; see [Quote unavailable](./quote-unavailable) and [My swap failed](./swap-failed).

## The balance shows 0 XRP right after a deposit

The app shows what you can spend. An account holding exactly 1 XRP, the reserve, shows 0 spendable: nothing is missing, it is the reserve. And a deposit of less than 1 XRP to a brand new account never arrives at all: the ledger refuses it and it stays with the sender, so the account keeps showing 0 and the activation card. See [The app says my account needs activating](./account-not-activated).

## Emptying the wallet completely

The 1 XRP reserve stays as long as the account exists. Closing an account is a separate XRP Ledger operation that the app does not offer, so in practice the last 1 XRP stays put. For multichain assets there is no reserve: Max sends the whole balance.
