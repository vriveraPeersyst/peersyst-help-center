## Check the address first

Tap **Receive** and look at the address. Compare it with the address that holds your funds (your old wallet, an exchange withdrawal history, or an explorer). If they differ, the balance is not missing: the app derived a different account from what you typed.

## Common causes

**A typo.** The app validates the phrase as a whole, and a single wrong word can still pass the check while pointing at a different account. Import again, carefully, one word per box, in order.

**Wrong format.** Secret numbers and a recovery phrase are two different backups for two different kinds of account. If your account came from Xaman and you have 8 rows of numbers, choose **Secret numbers**, not recovery phrase.

**A phrase from a different wallet.** Many people have more than one backup. Check the date or label on the paper.

**A 24 word phrase or a family seed.** XRP Mobile imports 12 word phrases and secret numbers. If your backup is a 24 word phrase or a family seed beginning with `s`, tell support: they will confirm what the app currently supports.

**The account is new.** If the address matches but the explorer says the account does not exist, it has simply never been funded. See [The app says my account needs activating](./account-not-activated).

## If the address matches and the explorer shows a balance

Pull down to refresh, check your internet connection, and give it a minute. If the app still shows zero while the explorer shows funds, contact support with both addresses: that is a display or connectivity problem on our side to look at, and your funds are safe on the ledger.
