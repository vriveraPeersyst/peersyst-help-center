## What is going on

The XRP Ledger lets anyone send any address a payment, and the fee is a hundredth of a cent. Some people use that to spray thousands of wallets with tiny amounts carrying a memo, a URL or a token name, hoping you look it up or visit the site. It is advertising at best and a phishing lure at worst.

Your wallet is not compromised. Receiving something proves nothing about your keys: your address is public by design.

## What to do

- Nothing. You can keep the dust or ignore it.
- Do **not** visit any website mentioned in the memo, and do not search for the token they are promoting.
- Do **not** try to "return" it or interact with the sender.
- If a memo claims to be from XRP Mobile, Peersyst, Ripple or an exchange, it is not. We never contact you through a transaction.

## Tokens you did not ask for

On the XRP Ledger a token needs a trust line, which only you can create, so unwanted tokens cannot appear in your XRP Ledger balance. If you see an unfamiliar token, check whether it is on another network held through the multichain layer, and if in doubt ask support before doing anything with it.
