## Restore on the new device

1. Install XRP Mobile from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Tap **Import wallet**.
3. Choose **Recovery phrase** if your backup is 12 words, or **Secret numbers** if it is 8 rows of 6 digits (the Xaman format).
4. Set a PIN for the new phone. It can be different from the old one.
5. Your XRP and your multichain balances come back, because both are derived from the same phrase. Repeat for each wallet you had.

## If the phone was stolen

The thief needs your PIN (or your face or finger) to open the app, and there is no way in without it. Still, if you have any doubt, move your funds to a new wallet: create a fresh wallet, back it up, and send everything to it. Once done, the old phrase no longer matters.

## If you have no backup

If the phone is gone and you never wrote down the phrase or secret numbers, the wallet cannot be recovered by anyone. Nothing about it was ever stored with us. If the phone is merely reset or the app merely deleted but you still have access to the device, contact support: depending on your phone's backup settings there are sometimes options, but do not count on it.
