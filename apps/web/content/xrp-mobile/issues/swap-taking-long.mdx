## Why it is slower than an XRP payment

An XRP payment settles in one ledger close, three to five seconds. A swap or a cross-chain send does more: it leaves the XRP Ledger, is routed through the multichain layer, and lands on another network, which has its own confirmation time. The screen tracks each leg with its own hash so you can see where it is.

## What is normal

| Route | Typical time |
|---|---|
| XRP payment on the XRP Ledger | seconds |
| Swap between XRP and a multichain asset | under a minute, sometimes a few |
| Send or deposit on Base, Arbitrum, Solana, Polygon | a few minutes |
| Send or deposit on Ethereum | a few minutes, longer when congested |
| Send or deposit on Bitcoin | 20 to 60 minutes |

## What to do while you wait

Nothing. Leaving the screen or closing the app does not cancel anything. When the last hash turns from pending to completed, the funds are there. You can paste that hash into the destination network's explorer to see it from outside the app.

## When to contact support

After 30 minutes past the typical time for the route, with the hashes from the transfer screen or Activity.
