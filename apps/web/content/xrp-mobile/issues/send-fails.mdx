## Check these four things

**1. Is the recipient account new?** A brand new XRP Ledger address does not exist until it receives at least 1 XRP. A payment of less than 1 XRP to it is rejected by the ledger. Send 1 XRP or more the first time.

**2. Does the recipient require a destination tag?** Exchanges and some services set their account to refuse payments without a tag. Go back, tap **Add destination tag** and enter the number from their deposit page. See [Send with a destination tag](/docs/xrp-mobile/send-destination-tag).

**3. Would the payment dip into your reserve?** You cannot spend the 1 XRP base reserve, and the network fee comes out of your spendable balance too. Use the **Max** button, which already accounts for both, or send a little less.

**4. Is the address correct?** The field turns green when the address is valid on the XRP Ledger. Valid is not the same as right: compare the first and last characters with whoever expects the payment.

## Why the app takes so long to tell you

When the ledger rejects a payment outright, the app currently keeps waiting for confirmation until it times out, then shows a generic error. The team is fixing this so the real reason appears at once. In the meantime, the four checks above cover almost every case.

## Not one of the four

Contact support with the details at the top of this page. If a payment shows as sent in Activity but the recipient disagrees, send them the transaction hash from the receipt: it settles the question on the public ledger.
