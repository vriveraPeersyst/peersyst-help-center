## What is going on

XRP Mobile is an XRP Ledger wallet, but this first version deliberately covers a narrow slice of the ledger: **sending and receiving XRP**, plus the whole **swap and multichain layer** through NEAR Intents (Bitcoin, Ether, stablecoins and the rest). Everything else the XRP Ledger can do is not in the app yet:

- Tokens issued on the XRP Ledger (IOUs held through trust lines, such as RLUSD or community tokens)
- NFTs
- MPTs (multi purpose tokens)
- The on ledger DEX and AMMs, escrows, checks, payment channels and the other amendments

If you imported an account from Xaman or another wallet that held any of these, they are **still in your account on the ledger**. The app does not show them and cannot move them yet, but nothing was lost or changed by importing.

## Check for yourself

Look your address up on an XRP Ledger explorer such as xrpscan.com. The **Tokens** and **NFTs** tabs list exactly what the account holds. That is the source of truth, and it does not depend on which wallet app you use.

## If you need to use them now

Your recovery phrase or secret numbers open the same account in any other XRP Ledger wallet. You can manage the tokens or NFTs there and keep using XRP Mobile for XRP and swaps: it is one account, and both apps see the same balance.

## What is coming

IOUs and other XRP Ledger tokens are next, and the plan is to keep adding XRP Ledger features and amendments release by release. Turn on notifications in the app to hear about it.
