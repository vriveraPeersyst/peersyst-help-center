## Why they can differ

- **XRP payments** are read from the XRP Ledger and appear on every device that holds the wallet.
- **Swaps and cross-chain transfers** are tracked by the device that started them. The same wallet on a second phone shows the XRP leaving, but not the swap card with its intents hash.
- **The hash on a swap card** may be the hash of the deposit leg, the payment that moved your XRP into the swap, rather than the swap itself. Both are real, and the explorer of NEAR Intents links one to the other.
- **A row that opens forever** or **appears twice** is a display bug being worked on. Neither changes anything on the ledger.

## What to trust

The explorer. Paste your address into xrpscan.com for XRP Ledger payments, and an intents hash into explorer.near-intents.org for swaps and cross-chain transfers. If the explorer and Activity disagree, the explorer is right, and support will use it too.

## If the explorer shows something you did not do

Stop and read [I think my wallet was hacked or drained](/docs/xrp-mobile/issues/wallet-hacked).
