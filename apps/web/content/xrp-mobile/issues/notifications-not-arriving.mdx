## The fix

1. In XRP Mobile, tap the gear, stay on **General**, and check the **Notifications** switch is on.
2. If the phone did not ask for permission when you flipped it, it already asked once and you said no. Allow it in the phone:
   - **iOS:** Settings, Notifications, XRP Mobile, Allow Notifications.
   - **Android:** Settings, Apps, XRP Mobile, Notifications.
3. Come back to the app. From here you hear about money arriving the moment it lands.

## Still nothing

- Notifications are per install. If you reinstalled, the phone asks again the first time you flip the switch.
- Focus or Do Not Disturb modes silence them.
- Notifications tell you about incoming funds and updates. They are not a substitute for checking Activity for a specific payment.
