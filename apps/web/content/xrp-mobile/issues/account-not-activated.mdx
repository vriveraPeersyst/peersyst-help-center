## What is going on

Creating a wallet in the app generates your keys and your address, but the XRP Ledger only creates the account when the address receives its first deposit of **at least 1 XRP**, the base reserve. Until that happens the ledger has never heard of your address, so it cannot send, swap or hold other tokens.

Nothing is broken. This is how every XRP Ledger wallet works, including exchanges and hardware wallets.

## The fix

1. Tap **Deposit** on the activation card, or **Receive** on the wallet home.
2. Pick **XRP** on the **XRP Ledger** network. Only that can activate the account.
3. Copy your address or show the QR, and send at least 1 XRP to it from an exchange, another wallet, or a friend. You can also tap **Buy** and pay by card.
4. The card disappears the moment the funds land, usually within a few seconds of the sending side confirming.

## Still showing after the deposit?

- Check the address you sent to matches the one in the app, first and last characters.
- Check the amount was at least 1 XRP **after** the sender's own fees. Exchanges sometimes deduct a withdrawal fee from the amount. A first deposit under 1 XRP is refused by the ledger and stays with the sender: it does not queue up, it simply never happens.
- Look the address up on an XRP Ledger explorer such as xrpscan.com. If the explorer shows a balance, pull down on the wallet home to refresh. If the explorer says the account does not exist, the deposit has not arrived on the ledger and the sender needs to check their side.
