## First, confirm it is a theft

Open **Activity** and compare with the account on an explorer such as xrpscan.com. Most "my funds are gone" reports turn out to be a hidden token, a swap that is still settling, or a payment the user made from another device. If there are outgoing payments you did not make, read on.

## Do this now

1. **Create a new wallet** in the app, back up its phrase, and move whatever is left to it. Do not reuse the compromised wallet.
2. If the compromised wallet was imported from another app or a browser extension, treat those as compromised too.
3. Do not follow any link, install any "recovery" tool or pay anyone who promises to get the funds back. Recovery scams target victims within hours.

## How it happens

- The recovery phrase, secret numbers or private key were typed into a website, a fake app or a form, or shared with someone posing as support.
- A "prize", "airdrop" or "verification" link asked to connect or confirm something.
- A phone or backup was accessible to someone else.

Nobody from XRP Mobile or Peersyst will ever ask for your phrase, and the app never sends it anywhere. The keys only exist on your phone.

## Can the funds be recovered?

No. Transactions on the XRP Ledger and on every other network are final. If the attacker also changed the account's signing keys (a regular key or a signer list, with the master key disabled), the account itself is lost even though the phrase still opens it in the app. Support can confirm exactly what happened from the explorer and help you secure a new wallet, but cannot reverse anything.
