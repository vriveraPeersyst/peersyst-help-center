## What is going on

An exchange does not give every customer their own XRP address. Everyone deposits to the same address, and the **destination tag** (a number the exchange shows next to the address) is what tells them the money is yours. The XRP Ledger delivers the payment either way. If the tag was missing or wrong, the XRP is sitting in the exchange's wallet with nobody to credit.

## First, confirm what actually happened

1. Open **Activity** on the wallet home and tap the payment.
2. Check the status is completed and copy the **transaction hash**.
3. Open the hash on an XRP Ledger explorer such as xrpscan.com. It shows the destination address, the destination tag that travelled with the payment, and the time.

If the explorer shows the payment delivered to the exchange's address, XRP Mobile did its part and the funds are at the exchange.

## The fix

Contact **the exchange's support**, not ours, with the transaction hash, the amount, the time, and the tag you used (or say it was missing). Every large exchange has a procedure for missing-tag deposits. Some charge a fee and some take weeks, but the funds are recoverable because they hold them.

We cannot recover it from our side: XRP Mobile never holds your funds, and a payment on the XRP Ledger cannot be reversed by anyone.

## If the receipt in the app does not show the tag

The app has a known display issue where a destination tag that was sent correctly is not shown on the receipt afterwards. The explorer is the source of truth: if the tag is there, it was sent.
