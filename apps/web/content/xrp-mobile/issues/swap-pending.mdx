## What Pending means

A swap is not a trade inside the XRP Ledger. Your XRP leaves your wallet to a deposit address, the swap is routed through NEAR Intents, and the asset you bought is credited to your wallet. The screen shows each leg with its own hash while they settle. **Pending means it is still moving, not that it is stuck.** Closing the app does not cancel it; the swap keeps settling on its own.

## Normal timings

- XRP to a multichain asset, or the reverse: usually under a minute, sometimes a few minutes.
- Anything involving Bitcoin or a congested network: can take longer, up to the confirmation time of that network.

## When to worry

After **30 minutes** with no change, or if the XRP left your balance and the destination asset has not appeared after that time.

## What the hash in the app means

The swap card shows an intents hash and the hashes of each leg. The first one can be the payment that moved your XRP into the swap, so it looks like a plain XRP payment on xrpscan.com. Paste it into explorer.near-intents.org and it links to the whole swap.

## What to do

1. Pull down on the wallet home to refresh. The balance often updates before the swap screen does.
2. Open **Activity** and check whether the outgoing XRP payment completed. If it did, the swap was accepted and is settling.
3. If it is past the 30 minute mark, contact support from Settings, then Support, with the hashes. The team can look up the swap on the intents explorer and see exactly which leg it is on. Swaps that cannot complete are refunded to the wallet they came from, in the asset you paid with. The refund is executed by the NEAR Intents team, not by us, and in the slowest cases it has taken weeks. You can also open a ticket with them directly, on Telegram at @near_intents_support_bot or through near.com support; a ticket from you and one from us in parallel has moved cases faster.
