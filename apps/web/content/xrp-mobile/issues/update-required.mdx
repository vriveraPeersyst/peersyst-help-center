## What to do

Open the App Store or Google Play, search for XRP Mobile and tap **Update**. Reopen the app and unlock as usual. Everything is where you left it.

## Nothing is lost by updating

Your keys stay in the phone's secure storage across updates. You keep the same wallets, the same PIN and the same settings. The only time you need your recovery phrase is when you move to a new device or reinstall.

## The store does not offer an update

- Stores roll releases out gradually; check again in an hour.
- Make sure you are signed in to the store with an account in a region where the app is available.
- As a last resort, uninstalling and reinstalling gets the latest version, but **only if you have your recovery phrase or secret numbers for every wallet**, because reinstalling removes the keys from the device.
