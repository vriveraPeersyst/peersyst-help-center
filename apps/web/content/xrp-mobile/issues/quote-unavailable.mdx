## The three usual causes

**1. The amount is right at the maximum.** Tap **Max** and then reduce the amount a little, or type an amount without long decimals. A quote needs a hair of room for fees and rounding, and this alone fixes most reports.

**2. The amount is under the minimum.** Every asset has a minimum per swap or send, shown on the screen (for Bitcoin it is a fraction of a coin, for stablecoins a couple of units). Below it no route exists.

**3. The route has no liquidity right now.** Swaps and cross-chain sends are filled by a network of solvers. When the market moves fast, or during a provider incident, some routes stop quoting for minutes or hours. Wait and try again. Assets that arrived from a rarely used network can stay without a route for longer.

## What it is not

It is not a problem with your funds. Nothing has left your wallet: a quote is a question, not a transaction. You can also try a different pair (for example swap to a stablecoin first) which often has deeper liquidity.

## Also check

If the token you want does not even appear in the picker, it is hidden, not unquotable: see [A token disappeared](/docs/xrp-mobile/issues/hidden-token).
