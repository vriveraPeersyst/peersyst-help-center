If anyone has seen you type your PIN, change it. It takes about twenty seconds, and the app makes sure it is really you first.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/change-passcode/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/change-passcode/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 6 screens and 34 seconds of video.

## 1. Settings, then Security

![Settings, then Security](/help/xrp-mobile/change-passcode/step-1.png)

Open the gear and move to the Security tab. This is where the PIN, your backup and deleting your data all live.

## 2. Tap Change passcode

![Tap Change passcode](/help/xrp-mobile/change-passcode/step-2.png)

Tap Change passcode.

## 3. Prove it is you first

![Prove it is you first](/help/xrp-mobile/change-passcode/step-3.png)

It asks for the PIN you have now before it lets you set a new one. Someone holding your unlocked phone still cannot change it blind.

## 4. Enter the new PIN

![Enter the new PIN](/help/xrp-mobile/change-passcode/step-4.png)

Now the new one. Four digits, and please not your year of birth or the code to your front door.

## 5. Confirm it

![Confirm it](/help/xrp-mobile/change-passcode/step-5.png)

Type it again to confirm. If the two do not match, nothing changes and you start over.

## 6. Changed

![Changed](/help/xrp-mobile/change-passcode/step-6.png)

Changed. The new PIN unlocks the app and signs your transactions from now on.
