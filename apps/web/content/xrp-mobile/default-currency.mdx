Tired of converting in your head every time you open your wallet? Put your own currency on the balance instead, and watch everything you hold follow it.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 6 screens and 48 seconds of video.

## 1. This wallet prices in dollars

![This wallet prices in dollars](/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/step-1.png)

Here is a wallet holding XRP, Dogecoin and a stablecoin, and every one of them is priced in US dollars. Say dollars are not your money.

## 2. Open Settings

![Open Settings](/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/step-2.png)

Tap the gear. Everything that changes how the app looks to you lives under General, and the current currency is right there on the row.

## 3. Code, name and symbol

![Code, name and symbol](/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/step-3.png)

Each row gives you the code, the name and the symbol, so there is no guessing between the dozens of currencies that share a dollar sign.

## 4. Search by name, not just code

![Search by name, not just code](/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/step-4.png)

The search matches names too. Type yen instead of J P Y and it still finds it. Tap the one you want.

## 5. Back to the balance

![Back to the balance](/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/step-5.png)

And this is the part worth seeing: go back to your wallet.

## 6. Everything follows

![Everything follows](/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/step-6.png)

Same coins, same amounts, priced in yen. Your holdings never changed: three XRP is still three XRP. Only the money you read them in did.
