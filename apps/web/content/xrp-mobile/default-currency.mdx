Prefer prices in yen, euros or dollars? Let's make XRP Mobile speak your currency.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 7 screens and 46 seconds of video.

## 1. Open XRP Mobile

![Open XRP Mobile](/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/step-1.png)

Open XRP Mobile. We start from a fresh wallet, and setup takes less than a minute.

## 2. Quick setup

![Quick setup](/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/step-2.png)

Create the wallet, name it, back up the recovery phrase and set your PIN.

## 3. Open your wallet

![Open your wallet](/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/step-3.png)

Open your wallet, skip the notifications prompt, and you're on the home screen.

## 4. Go to Settings

![Go to Settings](/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/step-4.png)

Tap the gear to open Settings.

## 5. Tap Default currency

![Tap Default currency](/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/step-5.png)

Under General, tap Default currency. Every entry shows its code, its name and its symbol, so you always know what you're picking.

## 6. Search by name

![Search by name](/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/step-6.png)

Search works by name too. Type yen and Japanese Yen pops right up.

## 7. Done, prices in yen

![Done, prices in yen](/help/xrp-mobile/default-currency/step-7.png)

Tap it, and done. The whole app now shows prices in Japanese Yen. You can change it back anytime.
