XRP Mobile is a mobile wallet for the XRP Ledger, available on iOS and Android and built by Peersyst.

- **Self custody.** The wallet generates your keys on your phone and keeps them there. Nobody else, including us, can move your funds.
- **XRP first.** Send and receive XRP natively on the XRP Ledger, with payments that settle in seconds for a fraction of a cent.
- **More than XRP.** Hold and receive Bitcoin, Ether, USDC and other assets from other networks, and swap between them and XRP without an exchange account.
- **Buy with a card.** A payment provider inside the app sells XRP straight to your address.
- **Explore.** Prices, charts and details for every listed token.

It is not an exchange and it does not hold your money. If you lose your phone, your recovery phrase restores everything on a new one.
