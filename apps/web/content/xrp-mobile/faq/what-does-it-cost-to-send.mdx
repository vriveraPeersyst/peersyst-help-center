---
title: "What does it cost to send XRP?"
description: "Sending XRP costs the XRP Ledger network fee, a hundredth of a cent, and nothing else. XRP Mobile does not add a fee on payments."
kind: faq
related:
  - "send-xrp"
  - "faq/what-does-a-swap-cost"
---

An XRP payment pays the ledger's network fee, currently about **0.00001 XRP**, a hundredth of a cent. There is no XRP Mobile fee on top. The amount screen shows what the recipient gets and the fee before you confirm.

For comparison, swaps and cross-chain sends have their own costs, because they involve other networks. See [What does a swap cost?](/docs/xrp-mobile/faq/what-does-a-swap-cost).
