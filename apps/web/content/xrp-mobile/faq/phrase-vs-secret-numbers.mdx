---
title: "Recovery phrase or secret numbers: what is the difference?"
description: "Both are a backup of an XRP Ledger account. A recovery phrase is 12 words and is what XRP Mobile creates. Secret numbers are 8 rows of 6 digits, the format Xaman uses. XRP Mobile imports either."
kind: faq

| | Recovery phrase | Secret numbers |
|---|---|---|
| Looks like | 12 English words in order | 8 rows (A to H) of 6 digits, 48 in total |
| Comes from | XRP Mobile and most wallets | Xaman (formerly Xumm) |
| Restores | XRP and your multichain balances | The XRP Ledger account |
| Error checking | The phrase validates as a whole | Every row has its own checksum |

**Which should I use?** Whatever your backup already is. A new wallet created in XRP Mobile always gets a 12 word phrase. If you are moving from Xaman, import with your secret numbers and the same account comes back with the same address.

**One current limitation.** A wallet imported with secret numbers cannot receive assets from other networks yet, only XRP. See [Known limitations](../issues/known-limitations). If you need multichain assets, add a second wallet with a recovery phrase.
