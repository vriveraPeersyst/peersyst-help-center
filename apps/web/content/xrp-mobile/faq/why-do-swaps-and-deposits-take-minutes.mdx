---
title: "Why do swaps and deposits take minutes?"
description: "Anything that crosses networks is settled by a bridge and solver network and needs the origin network to confirm. Most swaps take under a minute; deposits and withdrawals on slower networks like Bitcoin take longer."
kind: faq
related:
  - "issues/swap-taking-long"
  - "issues/swap-pending"
  - "issues/cross-chain-deposit-missing"
---

An XRP payment settles in seconds because it stays on the XRP Ledger. A swap or a cross-chain transfer leaves it, so it inherits the speed of every network it touches.

- The origin network must confirm the deposit. On Base or Solana that is quick; on Ethereum it depends on congestion; on Bitcoin it is tens of minutes.
- The settlement itself is fast, typically seconds once the deposit is seen.
- The withdrawal to the destination network again takes that network's time.

Leaving the screen does not cancel anything. If a transfer is pending well beyond the normal time for its route, the issue page [My swap is stuck on Pending](/docs/xrp-mobile/issues/swap-pending) says when and how to contact us.
