**Supported now**

- Send and receive XRP on the XRP Ledger, with destination tags.
- Create a wallet with a 12 word phrase, or import one with a phrase or with Xaman secret numbers.
- Buy XRP with a card.
- Swap XRP for Bitcoin, Ether, USDC and dozens of other assets, and hold, receive and send those assets on their own networks. This layer runs through NEAR Intents and needs no XRP or gas of its own.

**Not yet**

- Tokens issued on the XRP Ledger: IOUs and trust lines (RLUSD and others), NFTs, MPTs.
- The on ledger DEX and AMM pools, escrows, checks, payment channels, and the other amendments.

If your account already holds any of these, they stay on the ledger and are visible on any explorer; only the app does not show them yet. See [Where are my XRPL tokens?](/docs/xrp-mobile/issues/xrpl-tokens-not-shown).

**The plan**

IOUs and other XRP Ledger tokens come first, and the intention is to support the XRP Ledger's features and amendments progressively, release by release.
