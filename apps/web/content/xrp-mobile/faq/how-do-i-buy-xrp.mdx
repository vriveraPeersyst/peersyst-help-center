1. From the wallet home tap **Buy**.
2. Pick an amount or type your own. The screen shows roughly how much XRP that is at today's price.
3. Read the whole bill: the rate, the provider's fee, exactly how much XRP reaches you, and how long it takes. It is a live quote, so it recalculates if you change the amount.
4. Tap **Continue**. You are handed to the payment provider, outside XRP Mobile, for the card details. A first purchase usually includes an identity check.
5. When the provider completes the order, the XRP arrives at your address, which also activates a brand new account.

Your card never touches the wallet and neither do we. If a purchase is delayed or declined, the provider's confirmation email has the order id and their support contact; see [I paid by card and the XRP has not arrived](/docs/xrp-mobile/issues/card-purchase-missing).
