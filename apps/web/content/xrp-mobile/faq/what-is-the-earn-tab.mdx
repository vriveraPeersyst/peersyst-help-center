The Earn tab is deliberately empty of numbers: no rate, no launch date, no fine print, because there is no product yet. It explains what we are aiming for (XRP that works while it sits, keys that never leave your phone, no lock ups) and where we would start (XRP first, then RLUSD).

If you want to know the day it goes live, turn on the notification on that tab. Anyone claiming to offer "XRP Mobile staking" or "Earn rewards" today is not us.
