title: "Can I use XRP Mobile on a computer?"
description: "XRP Mobile is a phone app for iOS and Android. There is no web or desktop version. On a Mac with Apple silicon the iPhone app can run, with no guarantees."
kind: "faq"
related:

XRP Mobile is only distributed through the App Store and Google Play, and your keys live in the phone's secure storage. There is no browser version to sign in to.

If you need your funds on a computer, your 12 word recovery phrase can be imported into another wallet that supports the XRP Ledger. Do that only in software you trust, and never type the phrase into a website.
