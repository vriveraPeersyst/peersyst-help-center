Open XRP Mobile, tap the gear at the top right, move to the **Support** tab and tap **Start a chat**. The FAQ under it answers the questions we get most.

To get help fast, have these ready:

- Your **public address** (Receive, then copy; it starts with `r`).
- The **transaction hash** of the payment or swap you are asking about, from its receipt in Activity.
- The **asset and network** if it is not plain XRP.
- A **screenshot** of the problem.

Support will never ask for your recovery phrase, secret numbers, private key or PIN. If someone claiming to be support does, end the conversation and, if you shared anything, move your funds to a new wallet.
