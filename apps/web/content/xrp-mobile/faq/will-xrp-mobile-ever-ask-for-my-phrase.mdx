Never. The only place your recovery phrase or secret numbers belong is inside a wallet app you are restoring, typed by you.

Things that are always a scam:

- A "support agent" asking you to verify your wallet with your phrase.
- A website, form or bot that asks for your 12 words to "sync", "validate", "claim" or "unlock" anything.
- A message inside a tiny XRP payment pointing you to a site.
- Anyone offering to recover funds if you first give them your keys.

Our support team can help with almost everything using only your **public address** (it starts with `r`) and a **transaction hash**. If you have already shared your phrase with someone, create a new wallet, back it up, and move your funds to it immediately.
