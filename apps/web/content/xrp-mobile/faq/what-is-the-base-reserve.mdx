title: "What is the base reserve?"
description: "Every XRP Ledger account must keep 1 XRP on the ledger to exist. It is not a fee and nobody takes it; it is locked while the account exists, and the app shows your balance with it already deducted."
kind: faq

The XRP Ledger asks every account to hold a minimum balance, the **base reserve**, currently **1 XRP**. It stops people from filling the ledger with millions of empty accounts.

Three consequences you will see in the app:

1. **A new account needs 1 XRP to exist.** Until the first deposit of at least 1 XRP, the ledger has never heard of your address and the app shows an activation card.
2. **1 XRP is always locked.** The app shows the spendable balance, which is the ledger balance minus the reserve.
3. **Each trust line adds 0.2 XRP.** Holding a token issued on the XRP Ledger needs a trust line, and each one raises your reserve slightly.

The reserve is a rule of the XRP Ledger, not of XRP Mobile. You would see the same in any wallet.
