---
title: "Which languages and currencies does the app support?"
description: "The app ships in 13 languages and can price your balances in dozens of currencies. Both are changed from Settings, General, and neither touches your wallet or your keys."
kind: faq
related:
  - "app-language"
  - "default-currency"
---

- **Language:** tap the gear, stay on General, tap **App language**. Thirteen languages, fully translated, applied at once.
- **Currency:** tap the gear, stay on General, tap **Default currency**. The picker lists each currency's code, name and symbol and the search matches names too, so "yen" finds JPY.

Changing either only changes how the app reads to you. Three XRP is still three XRP.
