XRP lives natively in your XRP Ledger account. Every other asset (Bitcoin, Ether, USDC, Solana and the rest) is held by your **multichain account** on NEAR Intents, a cross-chain settlement network.

- That account is **derived from the same 12 word recovery phrase** as your XRP address. There is nothing extra to back up.
- Restoring your phrase in XRP Mobile restores both your XRP and your multichain balances.
- You never need gas or a wallet on those other networks. Depositing, swapping and sending is handled through the multichain layer, and the app shows every leg with its hash.

Wallets imported with Xaman secret numbers do not have a multichain account yet. See [Known limitations](../issues/known-limitations).
