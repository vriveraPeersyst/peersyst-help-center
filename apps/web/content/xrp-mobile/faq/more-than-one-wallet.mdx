---
title: "Can I have more than one wallet?"
description: "Yes. XRP Mobile holds as many wallets as you want, each with its own phrase and balance, and you switch between them by swiping on the home screen."
kind: faq
related:
  - "add-second-wallet"
  - "backup-your-wallets"
---

Swipe past the last wallet on the home screen to reach the add slide. From there you can create a new wallet or import one with a recovery phrase or secret numbers.

Things to know:

- Each wallet has its own backup. Backing up one does not cover the others.
- Each new XRP Ledger account needs its own 1 XRP reserve to be activated.
- Balances stay separate. Moving funds between your wallets is an ordinary payment.
- The PIN and biometrics are shared: they protect the app, not a single wallet.
