---
title: "Can I receive tokens from other networks?"
description: "Yes. Open Receive, pick the token and choose the network you are sending from. XRP always arrives natively on your XRP Ledger address; other assets are credited through the multichain layer."
kind: faq
related:

---

Tap **Receive** on the wallet home, pick the asset, then pick the network it is coming from. The app shows a deposit address for that asset on that network.

Three things matter:

1. **One asset, one network, one address.** USDC on Base and USDC on Ethereum need different addresses. Sending the right token on the wrong network, or the wrong token, can lose the funds.
2. **The address can change between deposits.** Always copy it fresh from the Receive screen.
3. **There is a minimum**, shown on the screen. Amounts under it are not credited.

XRP itself is always received directly on your XRP Ledger address, with the 1 XRP reserve rule for a new account.
