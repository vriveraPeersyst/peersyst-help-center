Typing a PIN every time you open your wallet gets old, and every time you type it in public someone could be watching. Use your face instead.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/biometrics/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/biometrics/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 5 screens and 37 seconds of video.

## 1. Settings, then Security

![Settings, then Security](/help/xrp-mobile/biometrics/step-1.png)

Open the gear and move to Security. Biometric authentication sits at the top, and it only appears if your phone actually has a face or a fingerprint enrolled.

## 2. Flip the switch

![Flip the switch](/help/xrp-mobile/biometrics/step-2.png)

Turn it on, and the phone asks you to prove yourself before it will accept the change.

## 3. Your face never leaves the phone

![Your face never leaves the phone](/help/xrp-mobile/biometrics/step-3.png)

Nothing about your face reaches XRP Mobile. iOS answers yes or no, and that is all the app ever sees.

## 4. Close the app to test it

![Close the app to test it](/help/xrp-mobile/biometrics/step-4.png)

Now close it properly and open it again. The wallet locks the moment it leaves the screen, which is what makes this worth doing.

## 5. It opens with a glance

![It opens with a glance](/help/xrp-mobile/biometrics/step-5.png)

And you are in, without typing anything. Your PIN still works whenever the face fails, so you are never locked out of your own money.
