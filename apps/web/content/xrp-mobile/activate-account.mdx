New wallet asking you to activate your account? Nothing is broken. Here is what the XRP Ledger wants, and why.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/activate-account/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/activate-account/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 6 screens and 72 seconds of video.

## 1. Your account needs activating

![Your account needs activating](/help/xrp-mobile/activate-account/step-1.png)

A new wallet opens on a card that reads Activate your account. Nothing has gone wrong. Your address already exists on your phone, but the XRP Ledger does not know about it until it holds some XRP.

## 2. Why the ledger asks for 1 XRP

![Why the ledger asks for 1 XRP](/help/xrp-mobile/activate-account/step-2.png)

Every account on the XRP Ledger keeps a base reserve of 1 XRP. It is not a fee, and it does not go to XRP Mobile or to anyone else. The amount stays yours, locked for as long as the account exists on the ledger, and it is released if the account is ever deleted.

## 3. Tap Deposit

![Tap Deposit](/help/xrp-mobile/activate-account/step-3.png)

Tap Deposit on the card. It opens the receive sheet, the same one you use for any incoming payment.

## 4. Pick XRP on the XRP Ledger

![Pick XRP on the XRP Ledger](/help/xrp-mobile/activate-account/step-4.png)

Choose XRP on the XRP Ledger. Only that network can activate the account. XRP wrapped on another chain will not arrive at this address, and can be lost.

## 5. Send at least 1 XRP

![Send at least 1 XRP](/help/xrp-mobile/activate-account/step-5.png)

Copy the address, or let the sender scan the QR code, and send at least 1 XRP from an exchange or another wallet. The first 1 XRP goes to the reserve, so send a little more than that if you want a spendable balance from the start.

## 6. Your account is live

![Your account is live](/help/xrp-mobile/activate-account/step-6.png)

The transfer usually lands in a few seconds. The activation card disappears, your balance appears, and Buy, Send, Receive and Swap become available.
