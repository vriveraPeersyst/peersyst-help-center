Need your XRP deposit address? Here's the safe way to find it, in seconds.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/receive/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/receive/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 6 screens and 54 seconds of video.

## 1. Open XRP Mobile

![Open XRP Mobile](/help/xrp-mobile/receive/step-1.png)

First, open XRP Mobile. For this demo we create a brand new wallet. If you already have one, you'll start from the home screen.

## 2. Quick setup

![Quick setup](/help/xrp-mobile/receive/step-2.png)

Name it, back up the recovery phrase and set a PIN. The wallet is ready in seconds.

## 3. Open your wallet

![Open your wallet](/help/xrp-mobile/receive/step-3.png)

Open your wallet, skip the notifications prompt for now, and you land on home.

## 4. Tap Receive

![Tap Receive](/help/xrp-mobile/receive/step-4.png)

Now tap Receive. XRP Mobile asks which asset you want to receive.

## 5. Search your asset

![Search your asset](/help/xrp-mobile/receive/step-5.png)

Type to search. Type xrp and the list narrows to exactly what we need.

## 6. Your deposit address

![Your deposit address](/help/xrp-mobile/receive/step-6.png)

And here it is: your QR code and deposit address. Only send XRP over the XRP Ledger network, and mind the 1 XRP minimum that activates a new account. Tap the copy icon and share it safely.
