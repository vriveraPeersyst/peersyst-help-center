Someone owes you XRP. Here is exactly where to tell them to send it, and the two things that go wrong if you get it wrong.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/receive/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/receive/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 5 screens and 47 seconds of video.

## 1. Tap Receive

![Tap Receive](/help/xrp-mobile/receive/step-1.png)

From the wallet home, tap Receive. Nothing here costs anything: an address is just where funds land.

## 2. Pick the asset

![Pick the asset](/help/xrp-mobile/receive/step-2.png)

Pick the asset you're expecting. XRP Mobile holds more than XRP, and every asset has its own deposit address, so choosing the right one matters.

## 3. One asset, one network

![One asset, one network](/help/xrp-mobile/receive/step-3.png)

Read the warning. This address takes XRP on the XRP Ledger and nothing else. Anything sent on another network is gone, and no one can get it back.

## 4. Your address and QR

![Your address and QR](/help/xrp-mobile/receive/step-4.png)

Here is your address, with a QR for anyone standing next to you. Copy it or share it, and always check the first and last characters against what the sender typed.

## 5. At least 1 XRP the first time

![At least 1 XRP the first time](/help/xrp-mobile/receive/step-5.png)

One catch on a brand new account: the first deposit has to be at least 1 XRP. That is the reserve the XRP Ledger asks for to let your account exist.
