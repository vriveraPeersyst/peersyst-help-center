Lose your phone and you lose nothing, as long as you did this once. It takes a minute, and the app makes you earn it.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/backup-your-wallets/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/backup-your-wallets/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 7 screens and 58 seconds of video.

## 1. Settings, then Security

![Settings, then Security](/help/xrp-mobile/backup-your-wallets/step-1.png)

Open the gear and move to Security. If your backup is still pending, the app has been telling you so with a red flag right here.

## 2. Pick the wallet

![Pick the wallet](/help/xrp-mobile/backup-your-wallets/step-2.png)

Choose which wallet you're backing up. Each one has its own phrase, so having done one does not cover the others.

## 3. Phrase or private key

![Phrase or private key](/help/xrp-mobile/backup-your-wallets/step-3.png)

You can export the recovery phrase or the private key. The phrase is what restores the wallet anywhere, so that is the one to write down.

## 4. A quiz first, on purpose

![A quiz first, on purpose](/help/xrp-mobile/backup-your-wallets/step-4.png)

Before it shows you anything, the app asks you to prove you understand what you are about to hold. This is not bureaucracy: people lose money to exactly these three questions.

## 5. Three questions

![Three questions](/help/xrp-mobile/backup-your-wallets/step-5.png)

Never share your private key. Nobody from XRP Mobile will ever ask for your phrase. And if you think someone has seen it, move your funds to a new wallet immediately.

## 6. Your PIN, again

![Your PIN, again](/help/xrp-mobile/backup-your-wallets/step-6.png)

Then your PIN, because passing a quiz is not proof that it is your phone.

## 7. Write it down, then close

![Write it down, then close](/help/xrp-mobile/backup-your-wallets/step-7.png)

And there it is. Write the words on paper, in order, and put the paper somewhere a thief and a fire cannot reach. Not a screenshot, not a note on your phone.
