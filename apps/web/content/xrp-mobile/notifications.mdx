You tapped I'll do it later when you set the wallet up, and now you only find out money arrived when you happen to open the app. Two taps fixes that.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/notifications/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/notifications/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 6 screens and 39 seconds of video.

## 1. Open XRP Mobile

![Open XRP Mobile](/help/xrp-mobile/notifications/step-1.png)

Open the app. Nothing you skipped during setup is lost: every one of those choices lives in settings afterwards.

## 2. Unlock as usual

![Unlock as usual](/help/xrp-mobile/notifications/step-2.png)

Unlock with your PIN, or your face if you turned that on.

## 3. Settings, General

![Settings, General](/help/xrp-mobile/notifications/step-3.png)

Tap the gear and stay on General. Notifications sits under your currency and your language.

## 4. Flip it on

![Flip it on](/help/xrp-mobile/notifications/step-4.png)

Flip the switch, and iOS steps in, because this permission belongs to the phone rather than to the app.

## 5. The phone asks, once

![The phone asks, once](/help/xrp-mobile/notifications/step-5.png)

Tap Allow. iOS only ever asks this once per install, so if you said no long ago the app cannot ask again and this switch would sit there doing nothing.

## 6. Now you'll know

![Now you'll know](/help/xrp-mobile/notifications/step-6.png)

Done. From here you hear about money arriving the moment it lands, instead of the next time you happen to look.
