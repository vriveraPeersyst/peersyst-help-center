Moving from Xaman? You do not need to export anything. Your secret numbers are all it takes.

Prefer to watch? The video walkthrough runs 72 seconds.

## 1. Tap Import wallet

![Tap Import wallet](/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/step-1.png)

Open XRP Mobile and tap Import wallet. Your account already exists on the XRP Ledger, so we are restoring it, not creating a new one.

## 2. Choose Secret numbers

![Choose Secret numbers](/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/step-2.png)

Choose Secret numbers. These are the 8 rows of 6 digits Xaman gave you when you backed up your account.

## 3. Type your 8 rows

![Type your 8 rows](/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/step-3.png)

Type them in order, from A to H. Take your time here, and never share these numbers with anyone.

## 4. Every row is checked

![Every row is checked](/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/step-4.png)

Each row carries a checksum, so the app confirms it with a tick as you go. If a row is wrong you will know right away.

## 5. Name it and set a PIN

![Name it and set a PIN](/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/step-5.png)

Give the account a name, then pick a PIN. The PIN unlocks the app on this phone and never leaves it.

## 6. Your account is back

![Your account is back](/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/step-6.png)

And there it is. Same address, same balance, restored from the same secret numbers Xaman uses.

## 7. Fund it to activate

![Fund it to activate](/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/step-7.png)

A brand new address needs at least 1 XRP to exist on the ledger. Tap Deposit to get yours.

## 8. Send XRP to this address

![Send XRP to this address](/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/step-8.png)

Pick XRP, and send at least 1 XRP to this address from any exchange or wallet, over the XRP Ledger network.
