If your XRP account came from Xaman, your backup is not twelve words. It's eight rows of numbers, and XRP Mobile takes them exactly as they are.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 6 screens and 49 seconds of video.

## 1. Open XRP Mobile, tap Import wallet

![Open XRP Mobile, tap Import wallet](/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/step-1.png)

Open XRP Mobile and choose Import wallet. Nothing is created here: you are bringing back an account that already exists on the ledger.

## 2. Choose secret numbers

![Choose secret numbers](/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/step-2.png)

Pick secret numbers rather than recovery phrase. Same account either way, just the format your backup happens to be in.

## 3. Eight rows of six digits

![Eight rows of six digits](/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/step-3.png)

Type the rows in order, A through H. Six digits each, forty eight in total, and the keypad moves to the next row on its own.

## 4. Each row checks itself

![Each row checks itself](/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/step-4.png)

A green tick means that row's checksum adds up. If one refuses to go green, you have a digit wrong in that row and nowhere else, which is the whole point of the format.

## 5. Name it and set a PIN

![Name it and set a PIN](/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/step-5.png)

Then a name and a PIN for this phone. The PIN is local: it guards the app on this device, it is not part of your account.

## 6. Same account, back

![Same account, back](/help/xrp-mobile/import-secret-numbers/step-6.png)

And your account is restored. Same address, same balance, and your secret numbers never left the phone.
