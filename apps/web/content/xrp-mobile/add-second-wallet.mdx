Keeping your spending money apart from the rest is easier when they are different wallets. XRP Mobile holds as many as you want.

<video controls preload="metadata" poster="/help/xrp-mobile/add-second-wallet/poster.jpg" src="/help/xrp-mobile/add-second-wallet/video.mp4"></video>

Prefer to read? The steps below are the same walkthrough, 3 screens and 27 seconds of video.

## 1. Swipe past your wallet

![Swipe past your wallet](/help/xrp-mobile/add-second-wallet/step-1.png)

Your wallets live in a carousel on the home screen. Swipe past the last one to reach the add slide.

## 2. Create or import

![Create or import](/help/xrp-mobile/add-second-wallet/step-2.png)

You can make a brand new one, or bring in a wallet you already own with its recovery phrase. Balances stay separate and you switch between them with a swipe.

## 3. Phrase or secret numbers

![Phrase or secret numbers](/help/xrp-mobile/add-second-wallet/step-3.png)

And here is the part only this screen offers: a new wallet can be protected by a 12 word recovery phrase, or by 8 rows of secret numbers, the format Xaman users already know.
